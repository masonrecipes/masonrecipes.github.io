# Red Velvet Cake with Ermine Frosting

## Ingredients

- 2¼ cups flour (regular, all purpose flour)
- 3 Tbsp cornstarch
- 3 Tbsp natural cocoa powder (unsweetened, best not to use dutch processed (see note 8))
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt (omit if using salted butter)
- 6 Tbsp unsalted butter (room temperature)
- ¾ cup unflavoured vegetable oil (I use canola)
- 1¾ cups white granulated sugar
- 3 large eggs (room temperature)
- 1 Tbsp vanilla essence/extract
- 1½ tsp white vinegar
- 3½ Tbsp red liquid food colouring
- ¾ cup buttermilk (room temperature)
- 1½ cups white granulated sugar
- ½ cup flour (regular, all purpose flour)
- 1½ cups milk (full fat)
- 1½ cups unsalted butter (room temperature)
- 1½ tsps vanilla extract/essence
- ⅛ tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) (see note 7 for convection oven) and grease and/or line two 8 inch cake tins.
2. Sift together the flour, cornstarch, cocoa powder, baking soda, baking powder and salt. Whisk or fork until well combined, then set aside.
3. In another bowl, cream together the butter, vegetable oil and sugar with an electric mixer for 2-3 minutes, until light and creamy.
4. Add the eggs one by one, mixing well for about 10-15 seconds between each addition.
5. Add the vanilla, vinegar and red liquid food colour, and mix until well combined. Set the mixer aside.
6. Fold half of the dry ingredients into the wet mixture with a spatula until just combined. Fold in all of the buttermilk, then fold in the remaining dry ingredients until just combined. Do not overmix.
7. Divide the batter evenly between the two 8 inch cake tins and bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean. Do not open the oven door too early, and avoid overbaking.
8. Cool the cakes in their tins for about 15-20 minutes, then turn them out onto a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.
9. Place a saucepan over medium heat and add the sugar and flour. Whisk or stir continuously for 2 minutes to gently toast the flour without letting it burn or change colour visibly.
10. Add 3/4s of a cup of milk (180g) and mix until smooth. Add the remaining 3/4s of a cup (180g) of milk and mix until smooth, using 1 and 1/2 cup of milk (360g) in total.
11. Stir gently until the mixture begins to bubble, then continue stirring for another minute, until thick and paste/pudding-like.
12. Cool the mixture to room temperature. To prevent a skin from forming, pour it onto a plate, cover it with cling wrap pressed against the surface, and freeze for 30 minutes.
13. Whip the butter in a large bowl with a hand or stand mixer on medium-high speed for 5 minutes, until light and fluffy.
14. Add the flour and milk mixture a tablespoon at a time while continuously mixing. Make sure the mixture is at room temperature, not hot or cold, to prevent curdling.
15. Add the vanilla and salt, and mix until well combined. Use the frosting once ready.

## Notes

- Do not overmix the cake batter, open the oven door too early, or overbake the cake layers, as these can cause sinking or dryness.
- The submitted recipe refers to note 1, note 2, note 6, note 7 and note 9, but their contents were not provided.

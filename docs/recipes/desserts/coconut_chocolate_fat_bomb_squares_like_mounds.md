# Coconut Chocolate Fat Bomb Squares (Like Mounds)

These are soft, melty, chocolaty bites with a crunchy coconut finish- great for those needing energy, satiety, or craving control.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 rounded cup coconut oil
- 1 ½ cups unsweetened shredded coconut
- 2 Tbsp dark cocoa powder (or dark cocoa)
- 2 Tbsp almond butter
- 2 Tbsp powdered sweetener
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (beaver free)

## Instructions

1. Melt coconut oil.
2. Stir in sweetener, cocoa powder, almond butter, and vanilla.
3. Add in coconut.
4. Pour into a 8x8 or whatever pan you want.
5. Freeze for an hour.
6. Slice into squares. Store in freezer.

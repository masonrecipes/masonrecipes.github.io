# Dill Dip

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup Hellmann's mayonnaise
- 1 tsp dill weed
- 1 tsp Beau Monde seasoning
- 1 tsp parsley flakes
- 1 tsp grated onion or minced dry onions

## Instructions

1. Mix ingredients together.
2. Let set overnight.

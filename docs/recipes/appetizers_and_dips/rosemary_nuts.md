# Rosemary Nuts

## Ingredients

- 4 cups mixed nuts (used cashews)
- 2 Tbsp chopped fresh rosemary (about 2-3 large sprigs)
- 2 Tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees.
2. Chop Rosemary.
    1. NOTE: we utilize a Nutra Ninja (or similar) cup attachment to chop Rosemary
3. Line baking sheet with parchment paper and set aside.
4. In a small bowl, whisk together the Maple syrup, olive oil, Rosemary, Smoked Paprika, salt, and Vanilla.
5. Spread the nuts on the prepared baking sheet and drizzle the maple syrup mixture over the nuts. Use a spatula to stir the nuts until they're evenly coated
6. Press into a flat, even layer and bake for 10 minutes, then remove from the oven and stir the nuts
7. Bake for an additional 5 minutes then remove from the oven and let cool

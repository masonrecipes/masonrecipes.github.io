# Taco Seasoning

## Ingredients

- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 4 Tbsp chili powder
- 1 Tbsp paprika
- 1 Tbsp salt
- 2 Tbsp cumin

## Instructions

1. In a mixing bowl, combine all the ingredients together!

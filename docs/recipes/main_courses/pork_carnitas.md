# Pork Carnitas

## Ingredients

- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- juice of 2 oranges
- juice of 2 limes
- 1 Tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- small pinch ground cloves
- kosher salt
- 1 medium white onion, quartered
- 6 cloves garlic, peeled
- 2 bay leaves

## Instructions

1. Add the chicken broth, orange juice, lime juice, cumin, chili powder, oregano, red pepper flakes, cloves, and 2 tsp kosher salt to a large slow cooker and whisk to combine.
2. Add the onion, garlic and bay leaves and stir to combine.
3. Add the pork and toss to coat.
4. Cover and cook on high until the meat is tender, about 5 hours.
5. Transfer the pork to a large bowl with a slotted spoon and discard the bay leaves, onion, and garlic.
6. Shred the pork into bite-size pieces with 2 forks, leaving some larger chunks, and season with salt and pepper.
7. Heat 2 Tbsp the lard in large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
8. Add half the shredded pork and press down into an even layer with a spatula. Cook, pressing occasionally with a spatula, until crisped and browned, 2 to 3 minutes.
9. Transfer to a serving platter and cover with foil to keep warm. Repeat with the remaining 2 Tbsp lard and shredded pork.
10. Serve with in a bowl with optional tortillas, tortilla chips, cilantro, jalapenos, tomatoes, guacamole. For Keto, serve over pork rinds.

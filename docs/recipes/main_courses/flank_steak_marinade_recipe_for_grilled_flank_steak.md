# Flank Steak Marinade Recipe (for Grilled Flank Steak)

## Ingredients

### Flank Steak Marinade

- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 Tbsp white vinegar, or use apple cider vinegar
- 2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 Tbsp honey, or use brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp spicy brown mustard, or use dijon mustard
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp hot sauce, or more to taste
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1\.5 lb flank steak

## Instructions

1. Whisk the soy sauce, olive oil, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, honey, mustard, garlic, hot sauce, and seasonings in a small bowl until smooth.
2. Place the flank steak in a wide pan or large plastic bag and pour the marinade over it, making sure the steak is covered. Massage the marinade into the steak with your hands.
3. Marinate the flank steak for at least 1 hour, or overnight for more flavor penetration. You can marinate it for 24 hours.
4. Heat a grill to medium-high heat and lightly oil the grates.
5. Remove the flank steak from the marinade and discard the marinade. Grill the steak for 3-4 minutes, then flip and grill for another 3-5 minutes, or until it reaches your desired doneness.
6. Transfer the flank steak to a plate or cutting board, cover it with aluminum foil, and let it rest for 10 minutes.
7. Slice the steak against the grain and serve.

# Flank Steak Marinade Recipe For Grilled Flank Steak

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons white vinegar (or use apple cider vinegar)
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon honey (or use brown sugar)
- 1 tablespoon spicy brown mustard (or use Dijon mustard)
- 4 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tablespoon hot sauce (or more to taste)
- 1 teaspoon cayenne
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1\.5 pound flank steak

## Instructions

1. Whisk the soy sauce, olive oil, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, honey, mustard, garlic, hot sauce and seasonings in a small bowl until smooth.
2. Place the flank steak in a wide pan or large plastic baggie and pour the marinade over it. Cover the steak and massage the marinade into it with your hands.
3. Marinate the flank steak for at least 1 hour, or overnight for more flavor penetration. You can marinate it for 24 hours.
4. Heat a grill to medium-high heat and lightly oil the grates.
5. Remove the flank steak from the marinade and discard the marinade. Grill the steak for 3-4 minutes, then flip and grill another 3-5 minutes, or until it reaches your desired doneness.
6. Transfer the flank steak to a plate or cutting board, cover it with aluminum foil and let it rest for 10 minutes.
7. Slice the steak against the grain and serve.

## Notes

- Use apple cider vinegar, brown sugar or Dijon mustard as alternatives where stated. Add more hot sauce to taste. Marinate overnight for more flavor penetration.

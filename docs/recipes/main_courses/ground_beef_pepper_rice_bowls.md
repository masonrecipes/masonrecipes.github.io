# Ground Beef & Pepper Rice Bowls

Serves 4

## Ingredients

- 1 lb. lean ground beef
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- ½ cup yellow onion, diced
- 1 cup green bell pepper, cut into thin slices
- 1 cup red bell pepper, cut into thin slices
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger root
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ⅓ cup beef broth
- 2 Tbsp honey
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp chili garlic sauce (Huy Fong)
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- Cooked rice, for serving
- Sliced green onions and sesame seeds, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Add ground beef to a large skillet over medium heat. Break into small pieces and cook until no pink remains. Drain the fat and set the beef aside, covered with foil.
2. Add olive oil and sesame oil to the pan and increase heat to medium-high.
3. Once the oil is hot, add the onion and bell peppers. Stir fry 4-5 min until tender.
4. Add garlic and ginger; cook 1 min until fragrant.
5. Return the beef to the pan and reduce heat to medium.
6. In a small bowl, combine soy sauce, beef broth, honey, cornstarch, chili garlic sauce, black pepper, and red pepper flakes; mix well.
7. Pour the sauce over the beef and vegetables. Cook 2-3 min until thickened enough to coat a spoon.
8. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds and serve over rice.

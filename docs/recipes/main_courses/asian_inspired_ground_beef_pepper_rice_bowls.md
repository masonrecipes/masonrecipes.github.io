# Asian-Inspired Ground Beef & Pepper Rice Bowls

## Ingredients

- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- ½ cup yellow onion, diced
- 1 cup green bell pepper, cut into thin slices
- 1 cup red bell pepper, cut into thin slices
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger root
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ⅓ cup beef broth
- 2 Tbsp honey
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp chili garlic sauce
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- cooked rice for serving
- sliced green onions and sesame seeds for garnish

## Instructions

1. Add the ground beef to a large skillet or wok over medium heat. Break it into small pieces and cook until no pink remains. Drain the fat and set the meat aside in a bowl covered with foil.
2. Add the olive oil and sesame oil to the pan and increase the heat to medium-high.
3. When the oil is hot, add the onion and green and red bell peppers. Stir-fry for 4-5 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
4. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 more minute, until fragrant.
5. Return the meat to the pan and reduce the heat to medium.
6. Combine the soy sauce, beef broth, honey, cornstarch, chili garlic sauce, black pepper and red pepper flakes in a small bowl or jar and mix well.
7. Pour the sauce over the meat and vegetables. Cook for 2-3 minutes, until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.
8. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds, then serve over rice.

## Notes

- Huy Fong chili garlic sauce works well.
